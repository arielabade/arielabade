I've spent more than five years acquiring customers through paid media, mostly for SaaS, e-commerce and
services. In most companies that work is split in two: one person runs the campaigns, and someone else
builds the models that say where the money should go. **I do both.** I run the campaigns, and I build the
models that decide where the next unit of budget goes.

At **Blocks**, a product-led SaaS with a marketplace and programmatic SEO, I led growth for about five
years. I managed paid media against gross margin, not against CPA or ROAS in isolation, and used logistic
regression to find out which channels actually produced recurring revenue. Before that I was a paid-media
analyst at the Atacama and BG9 agencies, and spent about three years with Axiom, a technical training company.

Today I run **ABADE**, my marketing analytics and paid-media consultancy. I build my own
campaign-optimisation and data tools on LLM APIs in Google Cloud. My background is a B.Sc. in Information
Systems from UFS, where I also did machine-learning research.

> answer directly to revenue.
